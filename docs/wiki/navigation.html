Returning to the app

By default, leaving locationjoystick returns you to Home. If you prefer to come back to the page you were using, you can change this.

Keep your current page

  1. Open Settings → Menus → Privacy.
  2. Turn off Return to home when backgrounded.
  3. Tap Save.

You can now switch to another app and back without being sent to Home. To restore the old behavior, turn the option on and save again.

What stays the same

  • Settings and first-time setup keep their pages even when this option is on.
  • A fresh launch still opens Home after setup.
  • Location simulation keeps running as before. This option does not start or stop it.
  • Opening a shared location or a notification shortcut can still take you to its destination.
  • This option does not hide screenshots or the preview shown in your phone's recent apps.

If your phone closes the app to free memory, some page details may need to be opened again. This option prevents the normal return to Home; it is not a backup of unsaved work.

The choice is included when you export your settings. Older backups keep the default behavior.
